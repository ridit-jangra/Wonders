Have an amazing idea that you and friend(s) always wanted to make? Then do it rn!

Wonders allow you to invite your friends onto your wonder and build it all together!!

# Invite someone

1. Open your friend and scroll down to **the team** section.
2. Hit **Invite someone :D** and you will get a code.
3. Send it to your friend(s)!!

# Join a wonder

Got a got from a friend? go to [Wonders page](/wonders), hit **Join a wonder**, type the code in and you're on the team. Your friend gets a DM from Panda when you join.

Some wonders are better with a friend (or four). You can invite people onto your wonder and build it together.

## What everyone can do

Everyone on a wonder can do these actions:

- Edit the name, description, thumbnail, demo link and GitHub link
- Write journal entries (you can edit your own entries, and everyone can read all of them)
- Ship it, ship it again, and unship it

Only the owner can delete the wonder (~~safety ✌️~~).

## Hackatime

Everyone connects **their own** Hackatime and picks **their own** projects. You'll only ever see your own Hackatime projects, never your teammate's hackatime.

## Shipping and surprises

Any of you can hit the ship button. It asks for **your** phone number, because every person on a wonder gets their own surprise sent to them.

The ship box also has a spot for each teammate's phone number. Add theirs if you know it. Don't know it? Ask them! If someone already added their own, you'll see that instead.

Reviews, changes requested and surprise updates get DM'd to everyone on the team.

## Leaving

Changed your mind? Open the wonder and hit **leave this wonder** at the bottom of the team box. The owner can also remove people from the team.


Now go make that wonder you always wanted to make with your friends :3

---
title: How review works
description: What happens after you hit Ship it.
---

After you ship, a real person on the Wonders team reviews your wonder. You'll see its status change on your dashboard:

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **building :)** | You're still working on it. |
| **in review :3** | It's shipped and waiting for (or going through) review. |
| **needs a fix :c** | It wasn't approved this time. Check the reviewer's note. |
| **shipped :D** | Approved! It's live on [Explore](/explore). |
| **reward incoming :3c** | Your surprise is on its way. |
| **wonder complete :3c** | All done. Thanks for making something wonderful! |

## What reviewers check

- The demo works and matches your description.
- The repo is public and the README explains the project.
- The commit history shows real, gradual work.
- Your Hackatime lines up with the project.
- It hasn't already been shipped to another Hack Club YSWS.

Review takes a few passes, so it may sit in **in review** for a little while. That's normal.

## If it needs a fix

If your wonder comes back as **needs a fix :c**, open it and read the note from your reviewer. Fix what they mention, save, and hit **Ship it** again. It goes back into review.

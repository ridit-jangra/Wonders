## 1. Log in with Hack Club

Head to the [dashboard](/dashboard) and log in with your Hack Club account. If you don't have one yet, you'll make one on the way.

Wonders asks Hackclub Club Auth for your name, birthday (to check if you're eligible :D), address (to ship your surprises to you *meow*). They're never shown to anyone else so you're probably safe 🥀 [~~i'm joking your not safe at all~~]

## 2. Tell us what you like building, doing, anything

The first time you log in we'll ask what you like doing they can be: hobbies, obsessions, weird niches, anything :3 It helps us get to know you.

## 3. Join #wonders

You'll be added to the [#wonders](https://hackclub.slack.com/archives/C0BS3VD47E3) channel on the Hack Club Slack. In the channel you can ask questions, share progress and hang out with everyone else making wonders.

## 4. Make your first wonder

Hit the big af [plus icon] icon on your dashboard. See [Making a wonder](/docs/making-a-wonder) for what to fill in.

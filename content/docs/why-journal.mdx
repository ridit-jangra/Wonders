---
title: Why should we journal?
description: Why keeping a journal while you build is worth it.
---

## Why?
Journaling helps the reviewers check the progress of your project, and they're not only for the reviewer, having a history of the progress of your project is something that when u see after u finish your project will be priceless, seeing all the progress, errors and the debugging took to complete that project feels magical to you and thats what matters, journals should feel magical to YOU, not even the reviewer can feel what you feel the moment you revisit those journals.

## I don't want other folks looking at my journals
Don't worry!! all of your journals are private and only a reviewer who reviews your project will see them, and our reviewers don't judge your progress :) it fascinates them seeing all the progress

## What to write in a journal?
Nope no format, write anything you want, the errors you fixed at 3am, the things you have to overcome to fix that issue, all of your research, every feature you added, bugs you fixed, what u felt after fixing that one bug that took you 3 hours to solve, literally anything!!

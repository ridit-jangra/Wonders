Some wonders are better with a friend (or four). You can invite people onto your wonder and build it together.

## Invite someone

1. Open your wonder and scroll down to **the team**.
2. Hit **Invite someone :D** and you'll get a code that looks like `ABCD-EFGH`.
3. Send it to your friend. Hit **copy** to grab it.

You can make a **new code** any time (the old one stops working) or **turn off** the code once your team is full. A wonder can have up to 5 collaborators.

## Join a wonder

Got a code from a friend? Go to the [Wonders page](/wonders), hit **Join a wonder**, type the code in and you're on the team. Your friend gets a DM from Panda when you join.

## What everyone can do

Everyone on a wonder can:

- Edit the name, description, thumbnail, demo link and GitHub link
- Write journal entries (you can edit your own entries, and everyone can read all of them)
- Ship it, ship it again, and unship it

Only the owner can delete the wonder.

## Hackatime

Everyone connects **their own** Hackatime and picks **their own** projects. You'll only ever see your own Hackatime projects, never your teammates'.

## Shipping and surprises

Any of you can hit the ship button. It asks for **your** phone number, because every person on a wonder gets their own surprise sent to them.

Reviews, changes requested and surprise updates get DM'd to everyone on the team.

## Leaving

Changed your mind? Open the wonder and hit **leave this wonder** at the bottom of the team box. The owner can also remove people from the team.

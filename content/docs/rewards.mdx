Every approved wonder earns a reward, and **it's a surprise**. We don't tell you what it is ahead of time; that's half the fun :3

## How it gets to you

1. Your wonder is approved and shows as **shipped :D**.
2. We start putting your surprise together and it shows as **reward incoming :3c**.
3. When it's done it shows as **wonder complete :3c**.

If it's something physical, we'll send it to the address on your Hack Club account, so keep that up to date at [auth.hackclub.com](https://auth.hackclub.com).

## 1. Log in with Hack Club

Head to the [dashboard](/dashboard) and log in with your Hack Club account. If you don't have one yet, you'll make one on the way.

Wonders asks Hack Club Auth for your name, birthday, address and phone. We only use those to check you're eligible and to send your surprise. They're never shown publicly; on Explore you only appear by your Slack name and picture.

## 2. Tell us what you're into

The first time you log in we'll ask what you're into: hobbies, obsessions, weird niches, anything :3 It helps us get to know you.

## 3. Join #wonders

You'll be added to the **#wonders** channel on the Hack Club Slack. That's where you can ask questions, share progress and hang out with everyone else making wonders.

## 4. Make your first wonder

Hit the big **+** on your dashboard. See [Making a wonder](/docs/making-a-wonder) for what to fill in.

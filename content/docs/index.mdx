Wonders is a Hack Club YSWS (You Ship, We Ship) where every project is a wonder and not a way to farm hours!

You make something that only could think of, or something you wanted to make for a while or just a random idea that seems cool to *YOU*

## Hours does not matter here

Focus on making something cool rather than looking at hackatime every few hours. Most programs count your hours but Wonders is different, we don't care how much time you spent making the project, we care how much you love this project, how much effort you put into it and most importantly how creative you were!
Build anything, a tiny wierd thing you saw in your dreams, a random but cool website you always wanted to make, possibilities are limitless you just gotta think :3

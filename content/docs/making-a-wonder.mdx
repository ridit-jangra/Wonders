## Create it

From your [dashboard](/dashboard) or the [Wonders page](/wonders), hit big af [plus icon] and fill in:
<img src="/docs-images/Plus-icon.png" alt="bleh"/>

| Field | What to put |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | What do you wanna call your wonder? 🤔. |
| **Description** | What is it? Why does it feel like you? *(I WANNA KNOW AAHHHHH)* |
| **GitHub repo** | A link to the public repo with your code. |
| **Demo** | A link where anyone can try it. *(Including you 😮‍💨 js saying 😏)* |
| **Thumbnail** | A picture of your wonder (up to 5MB). |

You only need a name, description & thumbnail to start. You can cook and come back with your links and fill them up!

## Ship it

When you're done cooking, open your wonder and hit **Ship it :D**.

> Before you can ship <br/> Your wonder needs a **thumbnail**, a **demo link** and a **GitHub link**. The Ship it button stays disabled until all three are filled in.

Once it's shipped it goes to review. See [How review works](/docs/review).

## Tips for a good review

- Make sure the demo link works in a fresh browser, logged out.
- Write a README that explains what the project is and how to run it.
- Commit as you go. Small commits over time are the best proof that you built it.

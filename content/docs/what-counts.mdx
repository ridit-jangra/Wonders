---
title: What counts as a wonder?
description: The wonder that could only come from you.
---

A wonder is a project you made that feels like **you**. It can be a website, a game, a tool, an app, a bot, an art thing, a music thing, something nobody's ever asked for. If it's code and it's yours, it can be a wonder.

## What we're looking for

- **It's yours.** You made it, and it shows something about you.
- **It works.** Someone can open the demo and actually use it.
- **It's real work.** The commit history shows it being built over time, not dropped in all at once.
- **It's explained.** The repo is public and the README says what it is and how to run it.

## What doesn't count

- Something you've already shipped to another Hack Club YSWS. Each project can only be shipped once across programs.
- Something you didn't make, or a tutorial project you followed step by step without making it your own.
- A project whose demo doesn't work or doesn't match what you described.

> Not sure? <br/ > Ask in [#wonders](https://hackclub.slack.com/archives/C0BS3VD47E3) before you start. It's always better to check first.

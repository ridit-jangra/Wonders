Wonders is a Hack Club You Ship, We Ship where every project is a wonder and not a way to farm hours.

You make something that could only come from you, you ship it, our reviewers take a look, and you get a surprise :3

<WonderSteps />

## Hours do not matter here

Most programs count your hours. Wonders doesn't. A tiny weird thing you're proud of beats a giant thing you dragged yourself through. What we care about is that it's **yours**, it's **real**, and it **works**.
